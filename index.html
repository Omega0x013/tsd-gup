Gup Forms & One Steps

for DBBAI Tang Soo Do.

My notes on the moves of and how to perform the forms and hand, feet, and self defence techniques I've needed to grade in Tang Soo Do.

The first section lists everything you'll need for your grading, grouped by the grade you're going for, rather than the belt you're currently wearing.

Example: A green belt is grading to green tag and an orange tag is double grading to green tag. They would both find the forms and one steps they're looking for marked under 'Green Tag'.

Forms

Key

Accompanying each move is two symbols: one for the direction you are stepping and one for the stance you end up in.

Direction Symbols

  • · - Not Moving.
  • ⠒ - Both feet together.
  • ↑ - Straight ahead.
  • ← - 90° to the left.
  • → - 90° to the right.
  • ↶ - 180° to the left.
  • ↷ - 180° to the right.
  • ↖ - 45° to the left.
  • ↗ - 45° to the right.
  • ↫ - 270° to the left.
  • ↬ - 270° to the right.
  • ↙ - 135° to the left.
  • ↘ - 135° to the right.
  • ↓ - Stepping backwards.
These directions are relative to the direction your chest is facing at the end of the previous move. For most moves in Horseriding Stance, the directions of your chest and head are not aligned.

Stance Symbols

  • C - Choon Be (Ready Stance).
  • S - Standing.
  • F - Front Stance.
  • B - Back Stance.
  • H - Horseriding Stance.
  • ∗ - Stances with a border around them are form-specific.
If you're not already familiar with the stances, then this resource will be dibsicult to parse. I strongly recommend asking your instructor to show you the stances in person.

Horizontal Lines

The horizontal lines indicate a place where you should Ki Hap. They often represent natural breaks in the form, and may be useful for teaching forms in stages.

Pyung Ahn Sam Dan

The Inside-Outside Block + Low Punch happen at the same time. To set up for it:
  • Bring your feet together so they are touching.
  • Bend your knees slightly.
  • Couch your punching fist against your ribs as you normally would.
  • Bring your blocking fist across your body to your hip on the same side as your punching fist.
  • Twist your upper body 45° in the direction of your punching fist.
Then, all you need to do is throw the block and punch simultaneously, bringing your upper body back to the center to generate power.

Hands

Hands 1-5

Starting Stance: Choon Be Jah Se
Attack: High Punch
  1. Block, Elbow - Step right leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Supported elbow strike to solar plexus.
  2. Block, Elbow, Elbow - Step right leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Supported elbow strike to solar plexus. Twist upper body to reverse elbow strike with left arm.
  3. Block, Knife - Step right leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Knife strike to throat.
  4. Block, Punch - Step right leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Shift into front stance, center punch.
  5. Block + Punch - Step right leg 45° out into horseriding stance. Simultaneously knife hand block with left hand and punch to throat with right hand.

Hands 6-10

Starting Stance: Choon Be Jah Se
Attack: High Punch
  1. Block, Triple Punch - Step left leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Two center punches. Shift into front stance, high punch.
  2. Knife + Knife - Step right leg out 45° into horseriding stance. Simultaneously knife hand block with left hand and knife to throat strike with right hand.
  3. Grab, Elbow - Step left leg back into back stance. Inside-outside knife block, grabbing opponent's arm. elbow strike to face.
  4. Block, Elbow, Backfist, Hammer Fist - Step right leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Supported elbow strike to solar plexus. Backfist to face. Hammer fist to groin.
  5. Punch, Block + Punch - Left center punch. Step right leg out 45° into horseriding stance. Simultaneously knife hand block with left hand and punch to throat with right hand.

Hands 11-15

Starting Stance: Choon Be Jah Se
Attack: High Punch
Hands 11-15 contains takedowns. Make sure to do these with control and help your partner to the ground, so they are not injured.
  1. Outside Block, Knee - Step left leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Grab opponent's shoulder, knee strike to stomach.
  2. Inside Knife, Knee - Step left leg back into back stance. Inside-outside knife block. Grab opponent's head, knee strike to head.
  3. Block, Knife, Knife - Step right leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Knife strike to throat. Twist upper body to reverse knife strike with left hand.
  4. Block, Takedown, Punch - Step left leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Grab opponent's shoulder, stomp on back of knee, taking them down. Kneel and punch solar plexus.
  5. Block, Sweep, Punch - Step left leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Step right leg behind opponent's leg. Sweep using right ridge hand strike. Kneel and punch solar plexus.

Hands 11-15

Starting Stance: Choon Be Jah Se
Attack: High Punch
  1. Outside Block, Elbow Elbow - Step left leg out into horseriding stance, outside-inside block. Elbow strike to ribs. Supported elbow strike to ribs.
  2. Grab, Two Finger Strike, Backfist - Step left leg back into back stance. Inside-outside knife block, grabbing opponent's arm. Two finger strike to eyes. Backfist to temple.
  3. Inside Block, Palm Strike - Step left leg out into front stance, inside-outside block. Palm strike to face.
  4. Reverse Grab, Pull, Punch - Step right leg back into back stance. Inside-outside knife block with right hand, grabbing opponent's forearm. Step out left leg into horseriding stance, in front of opponent's leg. Pull them down by the shoulder. Punch to side of head.
  5. High Block, Elbow, Arm Lock - Step left leg out into front stance, high block. Elbow strike to ribs. Lock opponent's elbow by bringing hands together above head with forearms either side, then bring downwards.

Feet

When performing side kicks, you may want to kick into the empty space under your opponent's arm, to the side of their body, allowing you to reach full extension without injuring them.

Feet 1-5

Starting Stance: Right Leg Back; Fighting Position
Attack: High Punch
  1. Front - Front kick center with right leg.
  2. Side - Side kick center with right leg.
  3. Round - Round kick high with right leg.
  4. Back - Spin ↷ 180° to the right on left leg. Back kick with right leg.
  5. Duck, Side - Step left leg left into horseriding stance, ducking your head under opponent's punch. Side kick with right leg.

Feet 6-10

Starting Stance: Right Leg Back; Fighting Position
Attack: High Punch
  1. Duck, Round - Step left leg across into horseriding stance, ducking your head under opponent's punch. Round kick with right leg.
  2. Push - Push kick center with right leg.
  3. Grab, Round - Inside-outside knife with left hand. Grab opponent's arm and round kick high with right leg.
  4. Grab, Side - Inside-outside knife with left hand. Grab opponent's arm and side kick center with right leg.
  5. Reverse Grab, Hook - Outside-inside knife with left hand. Grab opponent's arm and hook kick their back with right leg.

Feet 11-15

Starting Stance: Right Leg Back; Fighting Position
Attack: High Punch
  1. Outside Kick, Spinning Inside Kick - Outside-inside kick with right leg, blocking opponent's punch. Continue rotating 360°, inside-outside kick to head with left leg.
  2. Outside Kick, Side - Outside-inside kick with right leg, blocking opponent's punch. Without putting foot down, side kick center with right leg.
  3. Front, Round - Front kick center with right leg. Without putting foot down, round kick high with right leg.
  4. Inside Kick, Front, Round - Inside-outside kick with left leg, blocking opponent's punch. Front kick center with right leg. Without putting foot down, round kick high with right leg.
  5. Inside Kick, Round - Inside-outside kick with right leg, blocking opponent's punch. Round kick high with right leg.

Feet 16-20

Starting Stance: Left Leg Back; Fighting Position
Attack: Front Kick
  1. Low X Block, Punch, Knee - Step right leg back into front stance. Low X block. Grab opponent's leg and pull towards you. High punch with right arm. Right knee strike.
  2. Low Block, Reverse Hook Kick - Simultaneously step right leg back into horseriding stance, low block. Continue rotating, reverse hook kick with right leg.
  3. Low Block, Reverse Hook Kick, Round Kick - Simultaneously step right leg back into horseriding stance, low block. Continue rotating, reverse hook kick with right leg. Without putting foot down, round kick high with right leg.
  4. Leg Catch - Step left leg out into horseriding stance. Catch opponent's calf with right hand and thigh with left knife hand. Simultaneously step right leg behind opponent's leg, lift calf, and use left hand to push opponent's shoulder, taking them down.
  5. Low Block, Jump Kick - Simultaneously step right leg back into horseriding stance, low block. Jump and kick with right leg.

Self Defence

Self Defence 1-5 (Front Attack)

  1. Wrist Grab - Opponent grabs your left wrist with their right hand.

    Step right foot back into front stance. Twist clockwise and pull your left hand down to your belt, breaking your opponent's grip. Left knife hand strike to throat.

  2. Opposite Wrist Grab - Opponent grabs your right wrist with their right hand.

    Step right foot back into front stance. Sweep left knife strike to opponent's hand, breaking their grip. Right hand high punch to face.

  3. Shoulder Grab - Opponent grabs your left shoulder with their right hand.

    Swing left arm up and over opponent's arm, hooking your arm around their elbow. Pull upwards to lock opponent's elbow.

  4. Shirt Grab - Opponent grabs your left shirt or lapel with their right hand.

    Grab the heel of your opponent's hand with your right hand. Place left hand under opponent's elbow. Step right foot back into horseriding stance, twisting opponent's arm clockwise to pull them down.

  5. Belt Grab - Opponent grabs your belt from underneath using their right hand.

    Step left foot forward into front stance. Grab opponent's elbow with both hands and pull towards your chest, locking their elbow.

Self Defence 6-10 (Rear Attack)

  1. Belt Grab - Opponent grabs the back of your belt from underneath using their right hand.

    Step left foot back into front stance. Hook left arm behind opponent's elbow. Grab your own hand with your right hand. Pull your left arm forwards, locking their elbow.

  2. Collar Grab - Opponent grabs your collar with their right hand.

    Step left foot back into front stance. Grab the heel of your opponent's hand with your right hand. Place left hand under opponent's elbow. Twist opponent's arm clockwise to pull them down.

  3. Double Wrist Grab - Opponent grabs both wrists, holding your left arm out to your side and locking your right arm behind your back.

    Step back with left foot into front stance onto opponent's foot. Twist clockwise to free left hand. Pivot ↷ 180° into horseriding stance behind opponent. Grab right wrist with right hand, lock behind back. Pull shoulder towards you.

  4. Strangle Hold - Opponent places their right forearm across your throat.

    Step left foot behind opponent's leg into front stance. Left elbow strike to solar plexus, forcing them backwards.

  5. Head Lock - Opponent grabs your head with their right arm, pulling you down to waist height.

    Reach left arm behind opponent and over their shoulder. Knife hand strike to their throat, forcing them backwards.

Self Defence 11-15 (Side Attack)

  1. Shoulder Grab - Opponent grabs your left shoulder with their right hand.

    Grab the heel of your opponent's hand with your right hand. Place left hand under opponent's elbow. Step right leg forward into front stance and twist opponent's arm clockwise to pull them down.

  2. Wrist Grab - Opponent grabs your left wrist with their right hand (fingers back).

    Bring your left wrist up to your left shoulder. Reach under and grab opponent's thumb with your right hand. Break their grip and twist their arm anticlockwise, pulling them down.

  3. Handbag Snatch - Starting behind you, opponent walks past you and snatches your handbag.

    Grab your opponent (collar, shoulder, or wrist), pull them back, ridge hand strike to throat.

  4. Wrist Grab - Opponent grabs your left wrist with their right hand.

    Raise your right arm upwards, grabbing their wrist. Pull them in and side kick center.

  5. Side Hug - Opponent hugs you around the shoulders with their right arm.

    Step out 45° right into horseriding stance. Raise both arms and push outwards to break your opponent's grip. Grab opponent's head and knee strike.
